# New location ideas

Each location offers an open-ended drive with an easy route, an optional challenge, and landmarks that help players find their way without objectives or timers.

| Location | Driving features | Distinct landmarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Red Canyon** | An easy dry-wash loop leads to optional rocky shelf climbs and a winding rim trail. | **Split Spire:** two narrow towers mark the wash entrance. **Echo Arch:** a broad natural arch the car can pass beneath. **Ribbon Mesa:** a layered overlook reached by the rim trail. |
| **Old Quarry** | Terraced switchbacks connect a level gravel floor to higher ledges, with short rock-crawling detours. | **Spiral Cut:** the quarry's stepped central pit. **Iron Giant:** a retired excavator beside the main route. **Blue Seam:** a mineral band visible across several terraces. |
| **Dune Sea** | Soft dunes reward momentum, while a firm salt pan offers open, easy driving and a route home. | **Crescent Crown:** a tall curved dune. **White Pan:** the pale dry lake bed. **Stone Window:** a wind-carved opening on the desert edge. |
| **Autumn Woods** | Gentle leaf-covered lanes wind through trees, with a shallow creek crossing and a narrower ridge path. | **Cathedral Grove:** a circle of towering golden trees. **Copper Falls:** a small cascade beside the ford. **Foxglove Bridge:** a covered wooden bridge on the return loop. |
| **Island Tracks** | Compact headland loops offer grassy climbs, a sheltered shore road, and a fixed causeway to a second islet. | **Needle Lighthouse:** visible from most of the island. **Whale's Back:** a smooth rock ridge to drive over. **Tide Gate:** the stone causeway framed by tide pools. |
| **Mars Outpost** | A broad dust road circles a shallow crater; an optional rocky ascent reaches a relay station. Small crests allow playful, gentle hops. | **Habitat Seven:** a cluster of glowing domes. **Keyhole Arch:** an eroded red-rock passage. **Dish Ridge:** a high lookout with an oversized communications dish. |

**Suggested first addition:** Red Canyon. Its open wash supports relaxed exploration, while the shelf and rim trails give players more demanding routes. Mars Outpost would be a strong later addition with a more playful visual identity.

## Mars Outpost — expanded concept

**Scale and feel:** Mars would span **768 × 768 world metres**, half Northern Reach's length and width (and one quarter of its area). It would be a compact, continuous place to explore rather than a single crater scene. The tone is playful science fiction: recognizable Martian geology, a small human outpost, and a few oversized, friendly pieces of exploration hardware. The sky, lighting, and terrain provide the drama; there are no survival meters, damage, or required missions.

The outpost sits near the centre. Eight surrounding regions form a broad circuit, with trails radiating back to the outpost and cross-country shortcuts between them. Each region has a safe clearing for a regional start or reset. The main routes are wide enough to drive in either direction; harder rock, sand, and ridge lines are optional.

| Region | Place in the world | Distinct landmark | Driving experience and connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Habitat Seven** | Centre | Three low glowing domes around a circular landing pad, with a parked cargo lander nearby. | A flat, welcoming starting area. Broad tracks branch toward the crater, dunes, dry delta, and glass plain. |
| **Crown Crater** | North | A nested impact crater whose inner ring resembles a crown from the lookout. | Drive an easy outer rim loop or descend by a graded spiral to the crater floor. The east exit climbs toward Dish Ridge. |
| **Dish Ridge** | Northeast | An oversized communications dish above a line of smaller relay masts. | A switchback ascent offers wide views across the whole map; a lower traverse leads on to Glassfall Plain. |
| **Glassfall Plain** | East | A fan of dark, glassy streaks radiating from a small impact scar. | Long sightlines and firm tracks invite relaxed driving. The polished streaks make a short optional slippery crossing. |
| **Iron Maze** | Southeast | Tall, blade-like ironstone fins arranged around a broad central passage. | Weave slowly through rock corridors or take a smooth bypass around the fins. The southern exit reaches Keyhole Badlands. |
| **Keyhole Badlands** | South | **Keyhole Arch**, a huge eroded opening framing the distant outpost when viewed from the wash. | Follow a winding wash under the arch, then choose a gentle return or a rocky ledge above it. |
| **Fossil Delta** | Southwest | A branching network of ancient dry channels ending at a stepped sediment fan. | Channel floors give several navigable lines; a firmer upper-bank path links back to the outpost and Rust Dunes. |
| **Rust Dunes** | West | A tall crescent dune with an exposed dark bedrock island at its centre. | Loose dust rewards a run-up, while a firm perimeter track keeps the region accessible. The north trail reaches the lava tubes. |
| **Lava Tubes** | Northwest | A collapsed skylight reveals a broad passage and **the Quiet Vault**: a half-buried, angular structure of unknown origin with a faint glow beneath its seams. | Drive through the short, daylight-visible passage or over the surface ridge. A side alcove lets curious drivers approach the Vault without blocking the main trail; both routes reconnect before Crown Crater. |

**Route structure:** The outer circuit runs Crown Crater → Dish Ridge → Glassfall Plain → Iron Maze → Keyhole Badlands → Fossil Delta → Rust Dunes → Lava Tubes → Crown Crater. Habitat Seven supplies shorter spokes and return loops, so a player can sample one region or make a longer journey without following a prescribed route. Tall landmarks remain visible from neighboring regions and help with navigation alongside signs and the existing compass.

**The Quiet Vault:** Keep its origin unexplained. Its shape should stand apart from the outpost's rounded, practical equipment, while a small, steady light makes it noticeable from the skylight. It is a place to find and inspect, with no puzzle or required interaction.

**Natural boundary — the Great Ring:** Place the entire 768 × 768 metre world inside an ancient, eroded impact basin. This outer ring is much larger than Crown Crater, which remains a separate landmark within the playable area. The inner slopes rise gently enough to approach for views, then become steep, solid terrain near the map edge. Drivers can reach several rim overlooks but cannot accidentally drive into a void or over an invisible wall.

| Edge | Boundary character | Nearby region and view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **North** | A high, broken crater rim with broad shelves below its steep outer face. | Crown Crater's lookout faces the tallest section. |
| **East** | Fractured cliffs and fallen slabs, with a few apparent passes that curve back into the basin. | Dish Ridge and Glassfall Plain show the ring's layered rock. |
| **South** | Low badland terraces that build toward an impassable wall of weathered stone. | Keyhole Badlands has a reachable shelf for a view across the basin. |
| **West** | Dark lava escarpments and windblown dust banked against their base. | Rust Dunes meet the cliff foot; Lava Tubes open into the northwest flank. |

Beyond the rim, distant mesas and mountains fade into a dusty horizon. These background shapes give a sense of a larger Mars while the driveable terrain and its collision boundary stay within the Great Ring. The rim should vary in height and silhouette so it reads as geology rather than a uniform wall.

**New car — Mars Scout:** Add a third selectable four-wheel vehicle with an angular expedition cabin, a broad split windshield, wraparound side windows, chunky treaded tires, cream-white bodywork, restrained burnt-orange markings, an open roof rack, driving lights, exposed suspension details, and a small antenna. The user’s October 8 rover reference supersedes the initial bubble-canopy buggy concept. The design uses the game's shared four-wheel driving and surface handling; loose dust, rock, and slick glass create the different feel on Mars. The Mars Scout would be selectable in any location, with Mars Outpost featuring it by default.
