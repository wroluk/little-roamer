# Keyhole Badlands

Two asymmetric sandstone bridges grow out of rocky shoulders rather than standing
on a flat apron like constructed gates. The main rock rib extends 176 m east-west
around (0, 530), with a thin crown, uneven depth and unequal spring points. A smaller
companion at (103, 467) turns diagonally across the northeastern shelves.

| Feature | Placement and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Reset | (145, 575), 9 m; connected to Iron's west exit. |
| Low wash | Remains open beneath the main span and connects to the northern wash. |
| Main crest | Climbs from the western landing at (-88, 530), 18 m, to about 35 m before descending to (88, 530), 16 m. Narrow, unguarded and optional. |
| Crest approaches | Western spur leaves the existing lookout; eastern descent rejoins the regional start. |
| Ledge return | Existing western climb to (-92, 580), 24 m; the easy route does not require crossing the arch. |
| Gentle return | Low outer loop reaches Delta at (-190, 490). |
| Habitat shortcut | Northern wash at (0, 486) connects through the central basin to Habitat's courtyard. |
| Secondary geology | Smaller open arch, terraced buttes and low fractured slabs embedded in the arch shoulders. No trail posts. |

The bridge tops are actual collidable rock, not terrain grading across the openings.
Their bases extend into the surrounding terrain; the ramp shoulders stay below the
visible crown to avoid a concealed steep lip. Rendering and collision share the same
irregular meshes. Muted, horizontally clipped strata tie the arches to neighboring rocks.

The user's natural sandstone bridge photograph guided proportions and massing; it is
not used as a shipped texture. The current 250 m haze means the main arch frames the
northern basin, not a guaranteed view of Habitat.
