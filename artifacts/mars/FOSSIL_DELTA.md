# Fossil Delta

A broad stepped sediment fan around (-428, 434), descending from the eastern shelf toward two dry channels. The ground uses Mars's default rusty-orange sand, with the same natural variation and lighter route accents as the Habitat surroundings, rather than a separate beige regional tint. Thin pale sediment slabs and a stranded dark boulder provide contrast and distinguish it from Glassfall's black ribbons.

| Feature | Placement and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Reset | (-300, 505), 15 m; linked to Keyhole's western wash. |
| Northern channel | Forks at (-370, 470), descends northwest through the fan. |
| Southern channel | Sweeps southwest before rejoining the northern branch at (-505, 365). |
| Sediment shelf | Climbs to (-345, 405), 25 m, then returns to the channel junction. |
| Habitat shortcut | An unmarked road from the western courtyard crosses the central basin and meets the sediment lookout. |
| Exit | (-535, 280), 8 m, leading into Rust Dunes. |
| Stranded stone | Dark grounded boulder at (-458, 456), between the branches and clear of both roads. |

Every branch reconnects; no dead-end recovery or trail posts are required. Slabs have matching collision and deterministic chunk ownership.
