# Iron Maze — region card

Latest feedback removes trail posts from this wild region and its connectors. Stronger 55–220 m haze softens distant scenery pop-in; the fin field and screened approach retain their geometry.

Fin crests now use a narrow solid cap joined section by section. This removes the unintended deep roof trough between the two sides while preserving the irregular longitudinal silhouette, broad sediment feet and matching collision. Crest width is 8% of the base width, down from 22%; triangle count is unchanged.

Tall rust-black blades form an uneven roofline above open ochre corridors. The eastern Split Anvil is a pair of unequal, aligned fins; broad sediment feet and thin rusty seams explain their common geology.
