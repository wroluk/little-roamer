# Iron Maze — region card

Tall rust-black blades form an uneven roofline above open ochre corridors. The eastern Split Anvil is a pair of unequal, aligned fins; broad sediment feet and thin rusty seams explain their common geology.

| Feature | Placement and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Northern reset | (250, 144), 9 m; direct connection from Glassfall. |
| Main passage | Broad north–south route between the central blades and Split Anvil. Gentle grades and open junctions. |
| Western bypass | Low, smooth loop around the fin field; rejoins the southern passage. |
| Optional slalom | A narrower rock route bends between alternating fins with room to recover. |
| Split Anvil | Two unequal fins at (281, 190) and (301, 201), with a genuine open gap and matching triangle collision. |
| Fallen blade | Low tilted slab beside the western bypass creates an open side pocket. |
| Eastern lookout | (306, 260), 24 m; broad terrain shoulder looking north over the fins. |
| Southern connection | Turns west to (135, 290), reserving the next transition to Keyhole Badlands. |
