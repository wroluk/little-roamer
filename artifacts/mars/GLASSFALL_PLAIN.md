# Glassfall Plain — region card

A low ivory impact fan contrasts with Dish Ridge's red terraces. Three charcoal melt ribbons spread southwest from a fractured scar. The safe circuit stays firm; the short transverse crossing offers a deliberate taste of polished, slippery ground.

| Feature | Placement and purpose |

| Western reset | (195, -45), 8 m; arrival from Dish Ridge and Habitat Seven. |
| Firm circuit | Broad loop around the fan, rising gently to 12 m on the eastern edge. Connects north to Dish and south toward the future Iron Maze. |
| Impact scar | (282, -17); a broken raised lip surrounds a shallow floor and six clustered black impact rocks. A shared 10 m-radius melt pool beneath them connects continuously to all three flows. The southwest breach points into the glass fan. |
| Three solid melt flows | Continuous raised ribbons without sand-filled plate fractures. Rounded 1.8 m shoulders meet gently undulating 31–45 cm interiors; visible geometry supplies matching collision. |

| High eastern lookout | (314, -64), 18 m; a broad supporting shoulder overlooks the complete fan and keeps Dish in view to the north. |
| Secondary landmarks | Split pale shield slabs against the eastern boundary, low radial ejecta plates, and a paired stone threshold at the west arrival. |
| Outpost spoke | A firm diagonal road joins the existing habitat courtyard. |
| Southern exit | (260, 128), 9 m; ends before the unfinished Iron Maze region. |
